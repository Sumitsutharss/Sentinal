SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

The Emerging Problem

AI agents are transitioning from conversational assistants to autonomous actors with authority to execute financial transactions, access supplier APIs, procure physical goods, and manage resources.

Unconstrained AI autonomy creates catastrophic real-world operational and financial risk.

The Sentinel Solution

Sentinel acts as the gatekeeper between autonomous AI intent and real-world execution. Organizations define fine-grained boundaries: who the agent is, what categories it can touch, how much it can spend, and when human oversight is mandatory.
